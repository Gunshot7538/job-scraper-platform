How It Works

Welcome to JobFusion! Follow these simple steps to scrape jobs, match them with your resume, and land your dream role.

1
Enter Job Role

Type the role you're looking for, like "Software Engineer", "Data Analyst", or "Product Manager".

2
Set Location

Enter your preferred location — "Remote", "Mumbai", "US", or any city/country.

3
Select Platform

Choose from LinkedIn, Indeed, or Naukri — the scraper fetches live listings from your selected source.

4
Hit "Deep Scrape"

Click the Initialize Deep Scrape Sequence button. The AI engine starts extracting jobs in real-time.

5
Wait for Results

A loading animation will appear. This takes 15–60 seconds depending on the platform. Jobs appear automatically in the table below.

6
Upload Resume (Optional)

Upload your resume in the AI Candidate Profile section. The system extracts your skills and matches them against every scraped job.

7
Export & Apply

Download results as CSV, send yourself an email report, or click Apply on any job to go directly to the listing.

Pro Tip: Upload your resume first, then scrape. The AI will automatically rank every new job by how well it matches your skills — saving you hours of manual filtering!
